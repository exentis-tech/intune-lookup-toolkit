Start the local server

This page talks to your tenant through server.ps1, which reads your app registration from config.json:

  1. Copy config.example.json to config.json and fill in TenantId, ApplicationId and ClientSecret.
  2. Run Start-Lookup.cmd (or pwsh ./server.ps1).
  3. The browser opens on http://127.0.0.1:8089/.
